# Website Concept
STYLE_CONCEPT: A modern, clean fintech design that balances professionalism with approachability. The layout uses generous whitespace, structured sections, and soft, rounded elements for a premium yet user‑friendly feel. Subtle gradients and visual hierarchy make the interface engaging without clutter.
COLOR_SCHEME: A primary blue and white palette with soft gray accents. Gradients are used sparingly for buttons and highlights, creating a sleek, trustworthy aesthetic that resonates with fintech users.
TYPOGRAPHY: Inter font family throughout, with tight letter spacing and a clear typographic scale for headings, subheadings, and body text. Weight variations (regular, medium, bold) provide excellent readability and emphasis across sections.
UI_COMPONENTS: Pill‑shaped buttons, clean modular cards, icon‑driven navigation, and intuitive forms. Visuals such as dashboard mockups, device renders, and lifestyle imagery help ground abstract fintech features in real‑world usage. Fully mobile‑responsive with smooth scaling across screen sizes.
TARGET_AUDIENCE: Individual freelancers, expats, and businesses that need reliable, global money movement solutions. The visual tone inspires trust and professionalism while remaining friendly and approachable to non‑technical users.
