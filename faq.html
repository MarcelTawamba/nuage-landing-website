💳 How does a Nuage global account work?

With Nuage you get:

  • USD Virtual Accounts - U.S. bank account and routing numbers issued in your name.
  • SEPA Virtual IBAN - Euro-denominated IBANs for accepting SEPA payments.

With these, you can receive and send payments directly via ACH, Wire, SEPA from individuals or businesses in the USA and in the Euro zone.

No. Opening a USD, EUR account with Nuage is completely free. There are also no monthly maintenance fees.

✅ Supported vs. Unsupported Payments

Supported Payments:

  • Freelance platforms – Payments from Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer, Deel, PayPal, Wise, Amazon, Stripe, Airbnb.
  • Business payments – Transfers from a registered business for services provided or salary.
  • Family payments – Transfers from family members with the same last name.
  • Self-transfers – Sending money from your own your bank, fintech app, or brokerage account (e.g. PayPal → Nuage).
  • Friends & third-party payments – Transfers between unrelated people (up to $3,999 per transaction).

Unsupported Payments:

Nuage will suspend accounts if used for the following:

  • Refunds (from universities, businesses, etc.)
  • Gambling or darknet-related payments
  • Loans, debt repayments, or lending platforms
  • Adult services or other high-risk industries

❌ Why was my payment rejected?

Payments can be rejected for several compliance reasons, including:

  • Details mismatch – The sender’s info doesn’t match your Nuage account.
  • Payment recall – The sender marked the transfer as unauthorized.
  • Amount exceeds limit – The payment is higher than your account’s permitted limits.
  • Unclear relationship – No clear explanation of sender-beneficiary connection.
  • Unsupported US states – We do not process third-party transfers from Florida, New York, Louisiana, or Alaska.
  • Fraud suspicion – The transaction was flagged as potentially fraudulent.

If you believe your payment was wrongly rejected, you can request a manual review by contacting our support team.
